What it isn't.

It isn't a clinical manual.

It shows you how to think through common sporting injuries and how to talk about them in an interview. It won't give you a rehab protocol, and it won't teach you to manage a player pitch-side. Deciding whether a specific player is ready for Saturday isn't something you can read your way to. That's what we do together in a 1:1 session. Questions

Before you book.

Will this get me a job?

No one can honestly promise that. What I can do is help you understand what clubs actually look for, where your gaps are, and what your realistic next step is.

Do you make introductions to clubs?

I don't pass CVs on or make introductions, because that wouldn't be fair on the people I work with. The sessions are about getting you ready to make that approach yourself.

I'm a student with no experience. Is it worth it?

Often it's the best time. Most of what holds students back is not knowing what to prioritise — and that's fixable in an hour.

I'm not in the UK. Does that matter?

Sessions are online, so location isn't a problem. Registration, visa and qualification rules vary a lot by country, and I'll be honest about what I do and don't know about yours.

Can I get advice about my own injury?

These sessions are about your career rather than clinical care, so for an injury of your own it's best to see a physio in person.

What happens after I pay?

You get a confirmation straight away, then a personal email from me within 24 hours to agree a date and time. Sessions currently run Fridays 9am–6pm and Saturdays 9am–1pm, UK time, usually within a week or two. You also answer a few short questions so I can prepare. If it turns out I'm not the right person to help you, I'll say so and refund you.
